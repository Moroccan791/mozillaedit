## Method and timing caveat
Independent 48000Hz PCM, centered 1024-point/96-hop (21.3ms/2ms) spectra; four bands 30-180,180-800,800-3000,3000-12000Hz. Linear positive flux, each normalized by band-local +/-1s p95; HPSS proxy, not isolated drums. Zero-phase bandpass waveform RMS with 4-6ms smoothing and 10/20/50% rise estimates. Raw and 80ms-deduplicated attack ranks are both retained. They are heuristic salience, not calibrated loudness.
Original librosa onset_strength uses 92.9ms windows and 5.805ms hop, log-mel differences, global strength normalization. Local thresholding improves candidate recall, not acoustic identity/ranking. backtrack=False. Installed librosa explicitly shifts envelope by 46.44ms for centering compensation, but NOT a universal 46ms attack delay. Synthetic known 1.000s noise/bass attacks yielded envelope peaks 1.004263/1.010068s. Do not blindly subtract 46ms. Real selected events generally show ~20-35ms peak-to-attack displacement, with larger/multiple rises in first two.
