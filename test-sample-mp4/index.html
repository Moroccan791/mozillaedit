Slowed-song pacing (new — your request: “slow down the pace of gunshots”)

  • What it does: each clip keeps rapid-fire bursts rigid at 1x (gunshot transients stay crisp), and the gaps between bursts stretch with an eased curve so the next burst / the next cut lands on a measured attack of the song. Strength is computed per gap and bounded by a floor you choose (--slow-floor; 0.5 is the encoder’s limit).
  • Verified for these renders: every claimed landing is re-measured against the detected attack grid (exact to 1e-7 s); protected bursts are the original PCM at 1× (best alignment 0.00 samples; the residual is the 44.1→48 kHz resample, ≈ −55 dB); video and audio are retimed by the same certified per-row map.
  • Honest limits: the anchors are still unreviewed detections, the grid is measured acoustic attacks (not certified meter), and how much pace can change is bounded by the song’s attack spacing, the 0.5× encoder floor and how much song is left. QA stays warn-only. Rows that could not be beat-matched are reported as partial/rigid — nothing is force-fitted.
